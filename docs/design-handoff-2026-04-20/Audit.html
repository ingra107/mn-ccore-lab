Tasks table Dense, powerful, cramped

/tasks · pages/Tasks/*
  • P1
    Header stacks four distinct filter/view systems in one row: view tabs (Mine/All/Team), urgency tabs (Today/Week/Overdue), view-mode tabs (list/board/table), and a sort dropdown. Visual hierarchy collapses — everything shouts equally.

    FOCUS NEXT section at top: the three cards are low-contrast and visually equal-weight to regular rows below. The anchor section of the page should feel different from everything else.
    Render FOCUS NEXT as 3 substantial cards with more padding and gold left-accent; visually separate from the dense list with 48px whitespace.

    Every row is [Carried forward] — same issue as My Hub. Title column is swallowed by meta-prefix.

    OVERDUE section has 43 items. No secondary grouping (by project? by urgency?) means you scan a wall of dates.
    Sub-group OVERDUE by project once it exceeds 15 rows. Match the treatment THIS WEEK / LATER already uses.

    Urgent/High pills, due chips, and assignee dots are the right density for a table this dense. Don't lighten them.
