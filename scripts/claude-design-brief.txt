# Claude Design brief — MN-CCORE Lab Hub

## What this is
The MN-CCORE Lab Hub is the operating surface for Dr. Nicholas Ingraham's
critical-care research group at the University of Minnesota. It runs the
lab: tasks, meetings, projects, grants, manuscripts, trainee milestones,
and an AI research assistant named Hermes. Live at
https://mn-ccore-lab.pages.dev. React 19 + Cloudflare D1 + Hono API.
~19 lab members. Team launch is April 21, 2026 at the MNCCORE biweekly.

Full-name acronym: Minnesota Collaborative Critical care Outcomes and
Research Effort. Shortened: MN-CCORE or MNCCORE. Typeset with an ECG
spike (heartbeat) as the glyph between "MN" and "CCORE" in our wordmark.

## Design ethos — 5 rules we never break
1. **Operations center, not editorial magazine.** Dense information,
   readable. Utility over decoration. Columnar tables beat card stacks
   for data.
2. **Dark-first.** Background is a deep neutral #0b1017 (NOT blue-
   tinted). Light mode is secondary. Text is #e2e8f0, not pure white.
3. **One accent per view.** Teal for interactive, gold for emphasis.
   Max two non-neutral colors per surface.
4. **Three-tier typography, five-tier opacity.** Body weight 400, UI
   500, headings 600, metric displays 700 only. Opacity floor 0.30 on
   readable text.
5. **Zero monospace in content.** JetBrains Mono is for <kbd> only.
   Everything else is DM Sans. Fraunces only on public-marketing titles.

## Brand motif — the heartbeat line
Our signature is an ECG trace, used in the favicon and everywhere that
wants a brand moment. SVG path (use this literally):

  M0 32 L80 32 L120 32 L150 24 L175 8 L200 56 L225 16 L250 38 L275 28
  L320 32 L400 32 L440 32 L470 24 L495 8 L520 56 L545 16 L570 38 L595
  28 L640 32

Standard rendering: gold stroke (#c9a84c) 2px, glow filter ~2.5 blur,
baseline ghost at 0.18 opacity. Animates at 60 BPM via
stroke-dashoffset. On a dark background the glow reads as a live cardiac
monitor — that's the emotional register we want.

## Voice and tone
- First-person plural ("we") when talking as the lab.
- Direct, declarative. No hype. ("Runs the lab" beats "Empowers teams
  to manage workflows effortlessly.")
- Medical-professional register: confident, precise, never cute.
- Data-first framing: numbers over adjectives whenever possible.
